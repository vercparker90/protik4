Embertrail is a guide to eight destinations that glow on camera, sorted by the hour they shine. Made for travel creators and tour operators who film vertical.

Start at sunriseWork with us

Four rules for glowing footage.

Arrive early

Be on location 30 minutes before the light peaks. The best minutes pass quickly.

Shoot into the sun

Backlight makes dust, steam and hair glow. Expose for the highlights.

Frame for 9:16

Keep the subject centered and leave space at the bottom for captions.

Ask first

Respect people, sacred sites and local rules. Check drone laws before you fly.

For tour operators and tourism boards

We help you turn your best viewpoints into content that gets saved and shared. Three ways to work together.

01

Light scouting

We map sunrise and sunset angles, crowd peaks and access notes for your top filming locations and deliver them as one-page briefs.

02

Creator journeys

Hosted itineraries built around the golden hours, with time for retakes, clear deliverables and partnerships disclosed in every post.

03

Guide workshops

Short phone-filming sessions for your guides and drivers covering hooks, framing, captions and an easy posting routine.
